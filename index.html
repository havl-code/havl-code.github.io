my-network-scanner
$ sudo .venv/bin/python scanner.py 192.168.1.0/24
[+] ARP scanning 192.168.1.0/24...
[+] Host found: 192.168.1.1 | d8:9e:f3:11:22:33
[+] SYN scanning 192.168.1.1...
[+] Results saved to results.json
Why I built it
Tools like nmap do far more, but using them doesn't show you how they work. I wanted a small, readable scanner where each step, from host discovery to banner grabbing, is easy to follow.
What I learnt
ARP discovery, batched TCP SYN scanning, why UDP results are always less certain, rough OS guessing from TTL values, concurrent scanning with a thread pool, and saving partial results when a scan is interrupted.
